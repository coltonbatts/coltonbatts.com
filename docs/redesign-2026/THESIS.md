# Redesign Thesis — July 2026

## Visual thesis
A Fort Worth production bible pressed into carbon and vermilion — not a cream monograph performing taste. The site reads as a physical instrument: thick borders, filmic crop and grade on commercial work so Google/Nuuly/UA sit in the same ink world as the gothic hero, bone as secondary stock rather than default wallpaper. Authority comes from hierarchy, crop, and clarity of hire — not VOL stamps, archive theater, or numbered “studies.”

## Interaction thesis
1. Hero entrance as a hard cut (clip-path / physical displace), not a soft fade.
2. Work media warms on hover — grade and crop tighten like ink drying into the plate.
3. CTA / arrow nudge that feels mechanical, not gooey. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content plan
1. **Hero** — Colton Batts + one POV line + one support + Work / Contact CTAs + full-bleed dominant visual.
2. **Proof** — three featured projects (Google, Nuuly, Under Armour) with honest elevated roles and outcomes.
3. **Practice** — one sharp statement; film primary, design adjacent, tools as character.
4. **Tools** — one supporting beat (systems proof), not a second homepage.
5. **Clients** — quiet ledger.
6. **Close** — new-business ask with real clarity.

## Positioning lock
Colton Batts makes images and motion systems with a point of view — for brands and makers who want work that lasts.
Hierarchy: Film & Motion → Design → Tools.
